What this is

WebsiteTimeTrack is a free, open-source browser extension. You install it yourself from source. There is no service behind it and no account.

The extension is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It can lose measurements, miscount time or stop working after a browser update. Do not rely on it for billing, payroll or anything else that must be exact.

Blocking is not a lock

Limits and focus mode are a nudge, not security. Anyone can snooze them or disable the extension. Treat them as a reminder, not a guarantee.
