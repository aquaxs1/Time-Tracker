Terms of Use & Privacy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. What these terms cover

They cover the WebsiteTimeTrack browser extension and this website. Installing or using the extension means you accept them. If you do not, do not install it.

2. What this is

WebsiteTimeTrack is a free, open-source browser extension. You install it yourself from source. There is no service behind it and no account.

3. Privacy

Who is responsible

WebsiteTimeTrack and this site are run by Sebi. For anything about your data, message @aquaxs on Telegram.

The extension

What it records stays in your own browser. The extension makes no network requests — no server, no account, no analytics, no telemetry — so none of it ever reaches Sebi.

  • Times — how long you spend on each website (by domain), per day, kept for 365 days.
  • Sub-entries — with “Track sub-entities” (on by default), also the YouTube channel, GitHub or GitLab repository, subreddit, Twitch channel, X profile or Stack Overflow question, kept for 60 days. The YouTube channel name is read from the page; everything else comes from the address. Domains you add for section capture record the first part of the path.
  • Activity — that you are using a page (mouse, keyboard, scroll), so an idle tab doesn't count. Not what you type.
  • Settings and weekly reports — limits, categories, the ignore list, focus mode and your past reports.
  • Never — other page content, form data, passwords, or anything on sites in your ignore list.

Browser sync

Settings are kept in your browser's sync storage. If you are signed in to your browser's sync (with Google in Chrome, Mozilla in Firefox or Microsoft in Edge, for example), the browser copies them to your other devices through that provider, under its privacy policy; otherwise they stay on this device. “Sync usage data between devices” is off by default. Turned on, it does the same for your times of the last days you choose (14 by default, at most 30), per domain and in seconds. Turning it off removes this device's times from sync storage again.

Your copy, your control

  • CSV exports and JSON backups are files you save yourself — they hold the same data, so look after them.
  • “Delete” next to a site or “Reset everything” in the popup removes recorded times.

Vercel processes connection data (IP address, time, requested file, browser) to deliver the pages and protect them against abuse — legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Vercel is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • No cookies, no tracking — no analytics, no ads, no external scripts or fonts, and nothing stored in your browser.
  • GitHub — links to the source code and issues lead to GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA, also certified under the Data Privacy Framework), where GitHub's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy, and object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15–21 GDPR) — on Telegram, @aquaxs. Since the extension's data never reaches Sebi, that concerns the hosting data above. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Austria that is the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).
