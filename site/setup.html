Setup

First five minutes

Tracking starts the moment the extension is loaded. These four steps get the rest out of it.

  1. Pin it

    Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin WebsiteTimeTrack so the popup is one click away.

  2. Just browse

    Tracking starts immediately. Come back in an hour and the ranking will have filled itself in.

  3. Set a limit

    Open Settings, add a daily limit for the site at the top of your list, and tick block if you mean it.

  4. Use focus mode

    The Focus button in the popup blocks social and entertainment sites for an hour.
